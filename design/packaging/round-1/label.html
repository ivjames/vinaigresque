Vinaigresque. — packaging round 1 — direction label

Hypothesis: a label system first, a pouch second. One band carries the whole front — wordmark, descriptor, bottle mark, flavor with its icon, cue, net-weight slot — and is drawn so that it is the same artwork as a sheet label on a blank bag today and as the only printed area on a pouch later. The band is a field colour with a cream window: the field gives a sticker a deliberate edge on white or cream stock, and the window is where the wordmark keeps its sage period and the bottle keeps its cream ground, because neither survives on a dark field.

Scaling rule. Every dimension inside the band is a fraction of the band's height; width is air. So the four label sizes (3 × 2, 4.5 × 2.5, 5 × 3, 6 × 3 in) are one file with one number changed, and the same band drops onto the pillow pouch (3 × 2), the stand-up pouch (4.5 × 2.5) and the jars (5 × 3, 6 × 3) without re-setting anything.

Field colour
By family, never the identifier: herb blends on sage-ink, citrus blends on ink, pepper on burgundy. Two citrus flavors share ink on purpose — name and icon tell them apart, colour only assists.

Notes and open points

  • Contrast, measured. Cream on sage-ink 5.33 : 1; cream on burgundy 8.58 : 1; cream on ink 13.26 : 1; ink on cream 13.26 : 1 (wordmark, descriptor, back copy); sage-ink on cream 5.33 : 1 (back headings); muted on cream 6.56 : 1 (slot labels, guide tags). Not text: the sage period on cream 4.08 : 1, the cruet's taupe glass line on cream 1.46 : 1 (as on the site), and the cream window on white film 1.08 : 1.
  • Why a window. Sage on sage-ink is 1.31 : 1, so the wordmark's period cannot sit on the field; and the bottle's burgundy globs vanish on burgundy and go muddy on sage-ink. The cream plate is not decoration — it is where the two brand marks are legible.
  • Smallest type. At 3 × 2 in the net-weight slot is 6.5 pt and the cue 6.9 pt, above the 6 pt floor but with little to spare; the 3 × 2 is a prototype size, not a retail one.
  • Avery orientation is assumed landscape (3 wide × 2 tall) to keep all four sizes in one aspect family (1.5 : 1 to 2 : 1). Portrait use would need a second layout and is not drawn.
  • Icons. Two flavors have no ico-* file: Greek Herb and Ginger Citrus use the larger cutouts cropped into the coin. ico-dill has no flavor to serve.
  • Print realism. Avery stock is white; the plate's cream is then a printed tint, and on inkjet it may read as faint or as white. The hatched seal and gusset depths are working assumptions, not Pouch House or Vann's specifications.
  • Back in the sticker phase. The back content is drawn for the printed pouch. As a sheet label it does not fit a 3 × 2 in; which second label size carries it is open.
