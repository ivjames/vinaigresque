Vinaigresque packaging · round 1 · front

Hypothesis: the packet's unusually simple front, taken literally. Six items, cream ground, ink type, one picture. The wordmark and descriptor are the constant block at the top of every pack; the bottle is the constant picture; the flavor is its caption, in italic serif with one small ingredient beside it, so the family reads as one brand with six members and the top third of every pack is identical. No fields, no bands. Guides are dashed taupe and are not art. 1 in = 96 px.

4 · Stand-up pouch, back · 5 × 8 in

Vinaigresque.

Classic Italian Herb

Mix it

Three parts oil to one part vinegar. For the whole pouch, ¾ cup oil and ¼ cup vinegar. For one salad, 1½ tablespoons oil and ½ tablespoon vinegar.

Shake or whisk, then dress. There is no mustard in it, so it separates as it sits: shake again before serving.

In the pouch

Dried oregano, parsley and basil, garlic and onion powder, black pepper, salt, sugar.

Pair it

Extra-virgin olive oil and red wine vinegar are the house pairing. Or choose your own.

Vinegar · red wine, white wine or champagne, balsamic, apple cider, sherry, rice.
Oil · extra-virgin olive, mild olive, avocado, neutral.
