Directions

Empty the jar into a cruet. Add ¾ cup oil and ¼ cup vinegar — three parts oil to one part vinegar. Shake.

Vinaigresque.

Classic Italian Herb

Directions

  1. Add the mix to a jar with a lid or a cruet.
  2. Add ¾ cup oil and ¼ cup vinegar: three parts oil to one part vinegar.
  3. Shake until it comes together. Dress the salad and toss.
  4. There is no mustard in it, so it separates as it sits. Shake again before each serving.

One salad: 1½ tablespoons oil, ½ tablespoon vinegar.

Ingredients

Dried oregano, dried parsley, dried basil, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, salt, sugar.
