Method

  1. Measure. Three parts oil to one part vinegar, into something with a lid.
  2. Add the mix. [amount] of Vinaigresque for that much dressing.
  3. Shake. Lid on, hard, until it comes together. No whisk, no bowl, no spoon of mustard.
  4. Dress. Over the leaves at the last moment, and taste before you add more.

Why no mustard?

Nearly every vinaigrette recipe reaches for a spoonful of mustard to hold the oil and vinegar together. It is the usual emulsifier.

Vinaigresque leaves it out. That is the whole idea. [one or two lines on why that matters]
