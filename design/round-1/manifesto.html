01  The convention

Almost every vinaigrette has mustard in it.

Check the back of the bottle, or the recipe. It is nearly always there — and it is there for a job, not only for the taste.

02  The reason

Oil and vinegar won’t stay together. Mustard is what holds them.

Left to themselves the two separate within minutes. Mustard is the classic emulsifier: a spoonful binds oil and vinegar into one dressing that stays put. So it goes in the recipe — whether you wanted it or not.

03  This one

This one doesn’t have any.

Vinaigresque is a vinaigrette mix with no mustard in it. If you would rather it were not in your dressing — the reason is yours — this is the mix that leaves it out.

04  How to use it 3:1

Three parts oil. One part vinegar. Shake.

Three parts oil to one part vinegar, and [amount] of the mix. Shake, and dress. That is the whole method.
