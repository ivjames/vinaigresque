What it is

Most vinaigrettes contain mustard, and not for the taste of it.

Mustard is the emulsifier. It is what persuades oil and vinegar, which would rather not, to stay together for the length of a salad. Vinaigresque leaves it out. What you taste is the oil you chose, the vinegar you chose, and the mix — and nothing that tastes of mustard.

Why no mustard

Some people do not care for mustard in a dressing. Some cannot have it at all. [allergen statement — none until it can be made]

Either way, this is a vinaigrette in which mustard is absent rather than merely quiet. There is none to declare, and none to taste.
