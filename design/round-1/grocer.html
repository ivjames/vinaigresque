    Mustard is what most vinaigrettes lean on to hold together. This one does without it, so the dressing tastes of oil, vinegar and the mix, and nothing else is doing the work.

    Three parts oil, one part vinegar. No measuring beyond that, no recipe to look up, and it works with whatever oil and vinegar you already keep.
